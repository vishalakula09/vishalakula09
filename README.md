## Featured Projects

### 01 | NYC Yellow Taxi Analytics

**Python · MySQL · Power BI**

End-to-end analytics project examining **2.87 million NYC Yellow Taxi trips** from January 2024.

**Highlights**
- Analysed hourly pickup demand and borough-level travel patterns.
- Compared weekday and weekend taxi activity.
- Investigated trip distances, journey durations, and recorded charges.
- Developed a three-page Power BI dashboard to communicate findings.

**[View NYC Taxi Analytics Repository](https://github.com/vishalakula09/NYC-Taxi-Analytics)**

### 02 | Australian Hospital Emergency Department Analytics

**Python · SQL · Power BI**

Healthcare analytics project examining Australian emergency department demand, treatment timeliness, and hospital performance using publicly available reporting data.

**Highlights**
- Analysed emergency department presentations and demand patterns.
- Investigated treatment timeliness across triage categories.
- Explored four-hour departure performance and patient length of stay.
- Compared state-level and hospital performance indicators.
