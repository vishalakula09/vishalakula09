## Featured Projects

### 01 | NYC Yellow Taxi Analytics

**Python · MySQL · Power BI**

End-to-end analysis of **2,867,591 New York City Yellow Taxi trips** from January 2024, exploring travel demand, trip characteristics, and recorded financial activity.

**Project Highlights**
- Analysed hourly pickup demand and borough-level travel patterns.
- Identified peak pickup hours and weekday versus weekend differences.
- Investigated trip distances, journey durations, and recorded charges.
- Developed a three-page interactive Power BI dashboard.

### 02 | Australian Hospital Emergency Department Analytics

**Python · MySQL · Power BI**

Analysis of Australian emergency department performance from **FY 2011–12 to FY 2024–25**, using publicly available Australian Institute of Health and Welfare (AIHW) data.

**Project Highlights**
- Analysed national emergency department demand and historical presentation trends.
- Investigated patient treatment timeliness and four-hour departure performance.
- Compared patient durations and performance across Australian states and territories.
- Developed a three-page Power BI report with interactive financial-year comparisons.
