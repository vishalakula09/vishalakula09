## Featured Projects

### 01 | NYC Yellow Taxi Analytics

**Python · MySQL · Power BI**

Analysed **2.87 million NYC Yellow Taxi trips** from January 2024 to understand pickup demand, travel patterns, trip duration, and recorded charges.

**Key highlights:**
- Identified peak pickup hours and weekday versus weekend patterns.
- Examined trip characteristics and borough-level demand.
- Developed a three-page Power BI dashboard.

### 02 | Australian Hospital Emergency Department Analytics

**Python · SQL · Power BI**

Analysed Australian emergency department performance from **2011–12 to 2024–25**, examining demand, patient flow, and treatment timeliness.

**Key highlights:**
- Investigated national ED presentation trends and four-hour departure performance.
- Compared treatment timeliness, patient durations, and state-level indicators.
- Developed a three-page Power BI report.
